# _'Building your CV'_ Workshop
## Emilio Bruna, University of Florida

Below are links to some of the materials for the _'Building your CV'_ Workshop. 
This workshop was originally developed for graduate students in UF's Department 
of Wildlife Ecology and Conservation, and then briought back to life at the 
request of UF's award-winning [SACNAS chapter](https://sacnasfl.wixsite.com/ufsacnas). 

## Workshop Slides

You can scroll through the slides 
[here](https://brunalab.github.io/cv_workshop/slides/cv_workshop.html#/title-slide). 
To download the slides click the icon in the bottom-left corner (three lines) 
and then click "PDF Export Mode".
